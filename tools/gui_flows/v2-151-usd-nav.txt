# #151 the usd view's camera is Unreal Editor's viewport (RTX on the depot world):
# a left-drag turns and moves along the ground, a right-drag looks around, RMB held with
# W flies and the wheel sets the speed (kept in params.camera_speed when RMB comes up),
# a middle-drag and a left+right chord pan, the wheel dollies, Alt+left orbits the point
# under the press, Alt+right dollies along the view; a click still picks (#140), F frames the
# selection, then (nothing selected) the whole stage. Each step: a shot and a snap whose
# source.camera says where the camera went.
# Project: tools/gui_flows/v2-137-seed.py makes it (needs the rtx extra and the file).
resize 1600 900

# the loading cover stays up through the first frame (#156); a cold RTX start takes longer than the wait below
